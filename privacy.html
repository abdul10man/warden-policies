Warden Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Warden is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, security, utility, automation, and community management features for Discord servers.

2. Information Warden May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a Discord server, Warden may process Discord server information such as user IDs, usernames, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message-related information required for moderation or logging, and configuration settings.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide and operate Warden's features, including moderation, security, logging, configuration, tickets, and other functionality enabled by server administrators.

4. Data Storage

Some configuration and operational information may be stored so that Warden can continue functioning correctly after restarts. The amount and type of information stored depends on the features enabled by the server.

5. Third-Party Services

Warden may use third-party services when required for specific functionality. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Requests

If you have a question about information processed by Warden, you can contact the Warden administrator or the administrator of the Discord server where Warden is used.

7. Children's Privacy

Warden is intended to operate within Discord's platform and follows the applicable requirements of the services on which it operates.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Warden's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Warden project administrator.
